# Appendix Source Evaluation

The final source-evaluation authority is
`research/source-evaluation-summary.md`. It reconciles the original workbook
with the sources and claims actually used in the final report. The original
spreadsheet evidence remains at `research/Source_Evaluation_Tianlin_He.xlsx`.

The comparison explicitly considers:

- data frequency and market;
- volatility target and forecast horizon;
- model implementation and validation method;
- peer-review status and recency;
- relevance to this EPQ; and
- limitations when transferring a published result to this design.

Key examples include Patton (2011) for imperfect volatility proxies and QLIKE,
Dudek et al. (2024) for multi-model cryptocurrency comparison, Huang, Sangiorgi
and Urquhart (2024) for richer high-frequency neural-network evidence, and
Hansen and Lunde (2005) for GARCH(1,1) as a serious benchmark.
